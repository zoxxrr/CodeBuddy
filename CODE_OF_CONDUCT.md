# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful learning community.

Expected behaviour includes empathy, constructive feedback, patience with beginners, respect for privacy, and openness to different backgrounds and skill levels.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
